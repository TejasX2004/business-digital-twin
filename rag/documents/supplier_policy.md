# Acme Retail Pvt. Ltd. - Supplier & Vendor Payment Policy

## 1. Standard Supplier Payment Terms
- **Baseline Terms**: Standard terms for verified merchandise and logistics suppliers are Net 30 days from receipt of certified invoice and delivery verification.
- **Payment Schedules**: Accounts payable disbursements are executed twice monthly on the 1st and 15th business days.

## 2. Early Settlement Incentives
- **Early Payment Discounts**: Wherever negotiated, Acme Retail leverages 2/10 Net 30 terms (2.0% cash discount if payment is settled within 10 days of invoice).
- **Cash Condition**: Early settlement discounts may only be exercised when operating cash reserves exceed 2.5 months of anticipated operational disbursements.

## 3. Supplier Payment Delay Protocols
- **Authorized Extensions**: In periods of liquidity stress or unexpected working capital drawdowns, accounts payable disbursements may be staggered by an additional 15 to 30 days (extending settlement up to Net 60).
- **Vendor Relationship Safeguards**: Payment delays beyond 30 additional days require CFO notification and active communication with key suppliers to avoid credit holds, penalty interest, or shipping freezes.
- **Supplier Criticality**: Tier-1 primary product vendors must be prioritized for on-time payment to maintain supply chain continuity and secure volume rebate tiers.
