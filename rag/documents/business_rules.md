# Acme Retail Pvt. Ltd. - Core Business Rules & Financial Benchmarks

## 1. Profitability Benchmarks
- **Gross Profit Margin Target**: Acme Retail targets an operating Gross Margin of 30.0% or higher. The minimum acceptable Gross Margin threshold is 25.0%. Gross margins below 20.0% represent severe financial distress.
- **Net Profit Margin Target**: Baseline target Net Margin is 8.0%. A net margin below 4.0% requires immediate operational efficiency reviews.

## 2. Cash Runway and Liquidity Standards
- **Minimum Cash Runway Benchmark**: Acme Retail requires a minimum liquidity cushion of 3.0 months of operating expenses.
- **Liquidity Thresholds**:
  - Cash runway not reached in projection period: Cash position is sustainable and self-funding across the modeled forecast horizon.
  - Cash runway between 2.0 and 3.0 months: Acceptable operational buffer, requiring close cash monitoring.
  - Cash runway below 2.0 months: Critical liquidity shortfall triggering working capital rationalization.

## 3. Risk Level Definitions
- **Low Risk**: All core metrics remain within target policy boundaries. Cash runway exceeds 3.0 months, inventory coverage is between 1.5 and 4.0 months, and gross margin meets or exceeds 25.0%.
- **Medium Risk**: Indicates 1 to 2 minor variances from benchmark thresholds where financial buffers remain sufficient to absorb fluctuations.
- **High Risk**: Triggered when inventory coverage breaches policy (<1.0 month or >4.0 months), operating cash declines markedly against baseline, or receivables extend beyond 45 days.
- **Critical Risk**: Triggered by projected cash exhaustion within the forecast period, severe stockout exposure exceeding 15% of demand, or negative gross profit.
