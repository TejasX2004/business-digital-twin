# Acme Retail Pvt. Ltd. - Inventory Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope
This policy establishes authoritative inventory management benchmarks, safety stock targets, and reorder protocols for Acme Retail Pvt. Ltd. It governs all product categories across warehouses and distribution centers.

## 2. Safety Stock and Target Coverage
- **Target Safety Stock**: All standard inventory lines must maintain a baseline safety stock buffer equivalent to 1.5 months (45 days) of projected average demand.
- **Normal Operating Range**: Optimal inventory coverage is between 1.5 months and 3.0 months of forward sales demand.

## 3. Inventory Coverage Thresholds and Risk Levels
- **Critical Understock Risk**: Coverage below 0.5 months (<15 days). Represents imminent stockout danger, requiring immediate expedited replenishment.
- **High Understock Risk**: Coverage between 0.5 months and 1.0 month. Triggers priority purchase order creation to avoid lost sales.
- **Moderate / Normal Range**: Coverage between 1.0 month and 4.0 months. Inventory levels are considered healthy and aligned with operational needs.
- **High Excess / Holding Risk**: Coverage between 4.0 months and 6.0 months. Puts working capital at risk; initiates review of purchasing slowdowns.
- **Critical Excess Risk**: Coverage exceeding 6.0 months. Represents severe inventory accumulation, tying up critical liquidity.

## 4. Stockout Tolerance and Service Levels
- **Stockout Exposure**: Acme Retail enforces zero tolerance for stockouts on tier-1 revenue-generating items.
- **Lost Sales Threshold**: Any scenario generating projected lost sales or stockout exposure above Rs. 0 must trigger an inventory adjustment or supplier reorder review.

## 5. Inventory Turnover Expectations
- Minimum acceptable inventory turnover ratio is 4.0 times per year.
- Target inventory turnover ratio is between 5.0 and 8.0 times annually.

## 6. Inventory Expansion Guidelines
- When inventory levels increase, purchasing and inventory decisions must evaluate inventory coverage, forward customer demand, and operating cash availability.
- Inventory expansion must not push total inventory coverage beyond the 4.0-month policy ceiling.
- Additional stock commitments should be balanced against working capital liquidity to prevent cash lockup in excess holding.
