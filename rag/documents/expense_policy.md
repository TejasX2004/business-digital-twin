# Acme Retail Pvt. Ltd. - Operating Expense Policy

## 1. Operating Expense Framework
- **Budget Compliance**: All departments must operate strictly within approved quarterly operating expense (OpEx) budgets across logistics, facilities, administrative, and marketing categories.
- **Overhead Growth Ceiling**: Unplanned increases in operating expenditure exceeding 10% must be supported by projected incremental gross margin contribution.

## 2. Expenditure Authorization and Approval Limits
- **Department Discretionary Cap**: Branch managers and department heads hold discretionary spending authorization up to Rs. 50,000 for standard operational needs.
- **CFO Pre-Approval**: Any single unbudgeted commitment or recurring expenditure increase above Rs. 50,000 mandates written sign-off from the Chief Financial Officer (CFO).
- **Executive Committee Approval**: Capital commitments or long-term operational leases exceeding Rs. 5,00,000 require Board or Executive Committee approval.

## 3. Expense Containment Triggers
- **Margin Contraction Trigger**: In any quarter where gross profit margin drops below 25.0% or net margin falls below 5.0%, a company-wide freeze on discretionary expenses is automatically instituted.
- **Revenue Decline Safeguard**: If sales contract by more than 10% against baseline projections, department operating expenses must be audited and reduced by a minimum of 10% to protect operational solvency.
