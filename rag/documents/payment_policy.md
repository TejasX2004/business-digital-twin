# Acme Retail Pvt. Ltd. - Customer Payment & Credit Policy

## 1. Credit Terms and Payment Schedules
- **Standard Payment Terms**: Standard customer credit terms are strictly Net 30 days from invoice date for all vetted wholesale and commercial retail accounts.
- **Default Grace Period**: A maximum grace period of 15 calendar days is allowed beyond the due date (up to Net 45) before formal escalation.

## 2. Overdue Account Escalation Tiers
- **1 to 15 Days Past Due**: Automated electronic payment reminders and statement delivery to customer finance department.
- **16 to 30 Days Past Due**: Direct outbound collections outreach by accounts receivable team; temporary suspension of further credit line extensions.
- **Over 30 Days Past Due**: Formal written notice; full stop on new product shipments; review for dispute resolution or legal recovery.

## 3. Accounts Receivable & Working Capital Management
- **Target Days Sales Outstanding (DSO)**: Target DSO is 45 days or fewer.
- **Cash Flow Priority**: In periods of rapid sales growth, customer collection velocity must match or exceed delivery velocity. Payment delays beyond 30 days create a material working capital drag that reduces free operating cash.
- **Prepayment Requirements**: High-risk customers or customers exceeding their credit limits must make upfront payments or settle outstanding receivables before new orders are fulfilled.
